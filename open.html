Open a Course Notebook

Each link opens JupyterLab on our cluster: sign in with your cluster account, no VPN needed. It copies the course notebooks into your home, in the folder ospc-materials/notebooks, and opens the one you clicked.

Click the link again next time: it brings in my new notebooks and my changes, and keeps the changes you made. The files a notebook writes, like job outputs, stay where they are.

    The short form of each link is mturilli.github.io/ospc-materials/open.html# followed by the name of the notebook, for example open.html#scaling.
