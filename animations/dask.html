    About the measurements and the models

    Where. julia, a node of our cluster, 10 and 11 November 2026. A job with 4 CPUs and the default memory (3.77 GB), as you submit it. Data: 100 million synthetic taxi trips in 100 Parquet files, 1.92 GB on disk and 3.8 GB in pandas. Question: the mean fare of each pickup zone (263 zones). Times are medians of 5 runs, with the libraries already imported.

    Memory view. Measured: pandas reading every column, 22.8 s in a job with 3.77 GB, 6.4 GB pushed to swap; 2.34 s and 10.3 GB with --mem=16G. Dask with 4 threads, 1.04 s and 1.2 GB. The file-by-file growth in between is a schematic: pandas reads the files with pyarrow, then builds its own copy.

    Task graph. Simulated: 20 ms per load, 10 ms per sum, 5 ms per add, and a free worker takes the first ready task in Dask's order (finish a branch before starting a new one).

    groupby. Dask 2026.8 plans the question on 100 partitions as GroupByChunk, a shuffle into 3 partitions and Aggregate; with split_out=1, as a tree (TreeReduce, 8 at a time). The view draws 8 partitions and 6 zones.

    Chunk size. The model has one number, 0.2 ms for the scheduler to hand out each task, measured with chunks of 1,000 numbers (100,000 tasks, 20.4 s). It matches 1,000 and 1 chunks and is faster than the measurement for 100 chunks (0.07 s against 0.11 s).

    Data files: lecture18/measure (README.md, rep-j2-28219.out, lim-j-28394.out, with-import/rep-j-28212.out).
