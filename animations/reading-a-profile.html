    About this page

    The numbers are those of the review question about clean.py: they are hypothetical, chosen so that the listing adds up. One row takes 18.5 µs: 1.0 µs in <module>, 13.0 µs in fix_name and 2 × 2.25 µs in str.title. A real listing has more lines, for the functions inside pandas.

    The columns. tottime is the time spent in the function itself, without the functions it calls. cumtime is the time from the moment the function is called to the moment it returns, including the functions it calls. percall is tottime divided by ncalls. A real profile shows str.title as {method 'title' of 'str' objects}.

    See Appendix A of the Lab 1 handout (p. 10): look for the largest tottime and for the functions called once per row of your data.
